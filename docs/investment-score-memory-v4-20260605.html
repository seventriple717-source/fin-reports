行业全景

核心瓶颈

HBM capacity is sold out through 2026 and constrains AI accelerator shipments; conventional DRAM contract prices surged ~98% QoQ in Q1 2026 as HBM conversion eats wafer supply; NAND is tighter but recovering later. The three-player oligopoly limits competitive price destruction, but through-cycle normalization post-2027 remains the key valuation risk.

周期阶段

AI-Driven Supercycle Peak

关键洞察 (Key Insight)

The HBM shortage is structural through at least 2028 because each HBM stack consumes 3-5x the wafer area of conventional DRAM and SK Hynix commands 59-62% share with multi-year NVIDIA supply agreements locked. DRAM prices peaked Q1 2026 (+98% QoQ). Enterprise HDD is experiencing a parallel AI-driven nearline demand surge (data lakes for AI training). Pure Storage is the only software-led all-flash play with sticky Evergreen subscription model.
