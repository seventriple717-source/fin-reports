行业全景

核心瓶颈

Power delivery: transformer lead times now extend to 4 years with a ~30% supply deficit, constraining data center commissioning timelines and amplifying pricing power for switchgear, EPC, and grid hardware companies. Nuclear PPA demand exceeds supply, keeping baseload clean-power premiums elevated.

周期阶段

Infrastructure Build-Out — Cycle Peak Approaching

关键洞察 (Key Insight)

The AI power supercycle is real and multi-year, but capital markets have front-loaded the premium into the highest-visibility names (VRT, PWR, MPWR, BE, POWL). The best risk-adjusted opportunities are in the electrification infrastructure mid-caps — EME, CEG, HUBB, VST — where secular demand is already showing up in backlogs but valuations remain more disciplined. Pre-revenue nuclear specials (OKLO, SMR) warrant maximum caution.
