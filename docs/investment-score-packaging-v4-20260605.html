行业全景

核心瓶颈

CoWoS and 2.5D/3D advanced packaging capacity is the primary supply bottleneck for AI accelerator delivery through at least 2027. TSMC CoWoS lines remain sold out 18+ months forward despite a near-4x capacity ramp from ~35K to ~130K wafers/month by end-2026.

周期阶段

Demand Surge / Capacity Constrained

关键洞察 (Key Insight)

Advanced packaging has become the decisive competitive moat in semiconductor supply chains. TSMC's CoWoS monopoly on AI accelerator packaging, combined with structural outsourcing to AMKR and ASX, creates a tiered value-capture opportunity. ASP premiums of 5-10x traditional packaging, and the constraint is capacity and know-how.
